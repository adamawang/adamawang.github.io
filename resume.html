HubSpot

Technical Lead (2022–2026) · Senior Software Engineer (2021–2022)

I joined HubSpot as the first frontend engineer on Sandboxes and grew into Tech Lead for 3 senior engineers across the Sandboxes, Multi-Account Management, and Account Cleanup teams. Over five years, Sandboxes grew from a 20-user alpha to 40%+ of HubSpot’s enterprise install base, and its growth was still accelerating when I left.

My biggest project was Deploy to Production, which had long been one of the largest roadblocks for upmarket customers. I owned the deploy UI flow in close partnership with Product Design, and I designed the logic that finds asset dependencies and detects conflicts during a deployment. The launch unlocked significant recurring revenue and opened Sandboxes up to more enterprise customers.

I proposed and built a shared UI component library used by three frontend teams: Sandboxes, Multi-Account Management, and Partner Portability. It saved days of design and engineering time and kept the UX consistent across all three. I also built an internal debugging app end-to-end so customer support and non-technical users could triage sandbox issues themselves instead of waiting on engineering.

Most recently, I brought Sandboxes into HubSpot’s Breeze AI assistant with 10+ MCP tools and 3+ AI skills, so users can pull data and take actions through chat. I also used AI skills to automate content collection when new assets are onboarded to the Portability Framework, which cut down on manual work.

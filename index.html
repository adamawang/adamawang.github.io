About Me

I'm a full stack software engineer who builds products for enterprise customers. For the last five years I was at HubSpot, where I joined as the first frontend engineer on Sandboxes and grew into Technical Lead for three senior engineers. Over that time Sandboxes grew from a 20-user alpha to 40%+ of HubSpot's enterprise install base.

My biggest project there was Deploy to Production, a long-standing roadblock for upmarket customers. I owned the deploy UI and designed the logic that finds asset dependencies and detects conflicts during a deployment. I also built a shared component library used by three frontend teams, and most recently brought Sandboxes into HubSpot's Breeze AI assistant with 10+ MCP tools and 3+ AI skills.

Before HubSpot, I built enterprise video and digital signage products at Bunch, including a content scheduling system running on 10,000+ devices at Fortune 500 companies, and led development of the corporate website for Effective School Solutions. I started my career as an investment analyst before switching to software at Hack Reactor.

Outside of work I'm into motorsports and high-performance driving, photography, food, travel, and craft beer. I'm a lifelong fan of electronic music and love all things technology. Check out some of my projects on Github using the link below.
